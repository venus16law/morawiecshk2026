Hair Combing Ceremony · 上頭

Thursday, 8:00pm — family only, bride and groom in separate sessions.

The 上頭 ritual

The Hair Combing Ceremony is a traditional pre-wedding ritual in Chinese culture, usually performed the night before the wedding. The bride and groom each undergo the ceremony separately, often in their parental homes. A respected elder, such as a parent or close relative, combs the hair of the bride or groom four times, accompanied by blessings that symbolize:

  1. A harmonious marriage
  2. A long-lasting union
  3. Many children and grandchildren
  4. Longevity and prosperity

The ritual marks the transition from childhood to adulthood and prepares the couple for their new life together.

Your Responsibilities
Cocktail hour — 6:00pm to 7:00pm

This is the wedding party’s busiest window. Split up and cover the room:

  • Station yourselves at the room entrance to welcome and greet guests as they arrive
  • Direct guests to their table assignments — know the room layout and table numbers
  • Help organise the photo queue — guests who want a photo with V&M need to line up in an orderly way so the couple isn’t mobbed
  • Keep things moving and festive — guide people to drinks, introduce guests who don’t know each other, fill the room with good energy
Tea ceremony — 5:15pm

Be present and supportive. The order is Pete & Hope first, then Ruth & Marso, then Justin, then all aunts and uncles. Help keep the flow moving and make sure the next family members are ready to step in on time.

Justin Law — two specific cues
  • 7:00pm march in: Justin is walking in with the family and helps lead Stand by Me with the family group after Marso’s speech.
  • 7:10pm dinner prayer: Justin introduces Hope Morawiec to say grace before the first course begins. Be ready, know your cue.
During dinner

Once seated, your job is to enjoy the night — but keep one ear open. Cheer on the table toasts, support Francis Liu when he sings, and help Auntie Clara sign up singers if the queue slows down. If Venus needs help changing outfits, bridesmaids assist quickly and quietly.

Attire — same as LA 🎉

Good news — no shopping required. Bridesmaids, wear the same dresses as the LA wedding. Groomsmen, same tuxedos. Dress code for guests is formal attire, so you’ll fit right in.

Good to know
  • Aunts are stationed at the gifts table during cocktail hour — direct guests who are looking for where to leave gifts
  • Photographer and videographer arrive at 3:00pm — you may be photographed while getting ready
  • Champagne and high tea are provided while getting ready from 3:00pm
